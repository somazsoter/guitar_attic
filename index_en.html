Welcome to Guitar Attic Basel, a beautiful creative space for learning music. With my extensive experience as a guitar tutor, the studio is an ideal fit for both beginners exploring their first instrument and more advanced players who wish to develop their technique and theoretical knowledge.

Guitar lessons

I offer guitar and music theory lessons in Basel St. Johann for both beginner and intermediate players of all ages. In addition to learning about the foundations using a step-by-step approach, I strive to make your musical journey engaging and enjoyable by applying what you learn directly to real playing. Beyond the basic skills, I customize the classes to your individual goals whether that’s learning new songs, improvising, songwriting or anything else you would like to explore. Importantly, I also pay attention to ear-training along the way so that later on you can even learn music just by listening. In my lessons, you can discover a wide range of musical styles and techniques, from fingerpicking to high-energy rock solos.

On top of these, I also give piano and drum lessons for beginners. In case you’re not sure which instrument you want to learn, you even have the opportunity to try everything in the studio and pick your favorite one. I teach in English, German and Hungarian.

About me

My name is Soma and I’m a guitarist/multi-instrumentalist with over 10 years of teaching experience. Besides playing the guitar since the age of 13, I also formally studied music at the Szabolcsi Bence Music School in Budapest. Throughout the years, I have been part of many band formations and developed solid skills in music composition, live performances and studio recordings. My current music project ‘The Midnight Shower’ won the POP Stipendium in 2023 and has received numerous positive reviews on an international level.

If you would like to get an impression of my teaching style, I invite you to a free trial lesson, which you can book via WhatsApp or email below. Feel free to come by even if you don't have your own guitar yet. In case needed, I can even support you in buying your first instrument.
